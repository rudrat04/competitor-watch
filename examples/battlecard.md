# Northwind Metrics — Battlecard

## Overview

Product analytics platform for SaaS companies, from startups to mid-market. Sells self-serve with a sales-led Enterprise tier.

## Pricing & packaging

- **Starter:** $19/month, up to 5 seats
- **Pro:** $69/month, up to 25 seats (was $49 until 2026-11-09)
- **Enterprise:** custom pricing; SSO, audit logs
- Free plan: removed on 2026-11-09

## Positioning & messaging

- "The analytics stack that grows with you"
- Emphasises integrations (now including Salesforce export) and enterprise readiness

## Where they're strong / where we win

**Where they're strong**
- Broad integration catalogue
- Enterprise features (SSO, audit logs)

**Where we likely win**
- Setup time: our onboarding is faster for small teams
- Price: flat pricing is now cheaper for teams under 50 seats

## How to handle them in a sales conversation

- Ask how many seats they need in a year; Northwind's per-tier seat caps push growing teams up a tier.
- If they were on Northwind's free plan, offer a guided migration.
- Don't compete on integrations count; focus on time-to-first-insight.
