# Mindtickle — Battlecard

## Overview

Mindtickle sells an all-in-one revenue enablement platform; AI Sales Role Play is one module of it. Role plays are generated from each rep's skill gaps ("Behavior Intelligence™"), run against a dynamic AI buyer, and are graded by AI. It also runs a pharma field-enablement solution: AI doctor role plays, field-force certification on new indications, onboarding, and a shared "room" for dossiers with engagement tracking and consent capture.

- Who it's for: GTM and enablement teams in larger organisations (the pages cite Cisco and a regional VP for Australia & New Zealand), plus pharma field teams.

## Positioning & messaging

- "The Leading AI Sales Role Play Provider": win more deals with hyper-realistic scenarios and immediate feedback.
- Scale without managers as the bottleneck: "grade thousands of submissions in minutes".
- Proof points on the page: Cisco "increased deal sizes by 31%", and a customer quote about saving "38 weeks of manager time".
- Platform story: Behavior Intelligence™, an agentic framework, and an interoperable "headless" architecture.
- Pharma: "Bespoke enablement for your field teams", with an AI specialist "who interrupts, pushes back on data, just like a real doctor"; HIPAA, ISO 42001:2023 and EU AI Act compliance claims.

## Where they're strong / where we win

**Where they're strong**
- Breadth: role play is part of a full enablement suite (onboarding, content, conversation intelligence, deal guides), so it can be bought as one platform.
- Personalisation from real performance data: role plays are built from each rep's skill gaps.
- Scale claims and a big-logo proof point (Cisco, 31% larger deals).
- Languages: role plays and feedback in "25+ languages", plus screen-share role plays for demos.
- Pharma-specific features: doctor role plays, certification on new indications, compliant content sharing.

**Where Rehearsely likely wins**
- India focus: the watched pages show no India, BFSI or vernacular-specific messaging; Hindi/Hinglish personas are likely a sharper fit than a generic "25+ languages" claim.
- Regulated Indian BFSI: no BFSI page or scenarios were observed; compliance-aware Indian banking and insurance scenarios are likely a gap for them.
- Focus and speed: a point solution is likely faster to roll out than a platform built around Behavior Intelligence™ and an agentic framework.
- Commercials: no public pricing was observed; INR pricing is likely an advantage with Indian buyers.

## How to handle them in a sales conversation

- Ask whether they need a whole enablement platform or a roleplay and coaching tool for branch and field reps. Lead with focus and time-to-value if it's the latter.
- Demo a Hinglish roleplay with a branch customer or a doctor. Their pages claim "25+ languages" but show nothing India-specific, so make them prove vernacular quality.
- In BFSI, bring compliant Indian scenarios (e.g. mis-selling guardrails for insurance or loans). Their watched pages show a pharma page but no BFSI one.
- In pharma, expect them to lead with AI doctor role plays and HIPAA/EU AI Act claims; counter with India-specific doctor personas and mobile-first practice for field reps.
- If Cisco's "31% larger deals" comes up, agree the outcome matters, then ask for a reference in Indian BFSI or pharma.
- Pricing: they show none publicly, so lead with transparent INR pricing.
