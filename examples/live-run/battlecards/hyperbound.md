# Hyperbound — Battlecard

## Overview

Hyperbound is an AI sales coach in two products: **Practice** (AI sales roleplays, custom buyer bots, scorecards, coaching) and **Perform** (real-call scoring, deal coaching, CRM and conversation intelligence), plus "Kota", an AI sales performance agent.

- Who it's for: B2B sales teams (SDRs, AEs) and, on the banking page, financial advisors: prospecting high-net-worth clients, fee objections, asset consolidation and emotional client conversations.

## Pricing & packaging

- **Model:** "priced per user, with separate licenses available for Practice and Perform". No prices are shown.
- **Plans:** Free, Practice (Enterprise) and Perform ("Recommended", Enterprise; "Everything in Practice" plus real call scoring, deal coaching, CRM and conversation intelligence, bite-sized roleplays, call capture).
- **Free plan:** includes the Outbound, Objection Handling and Inbound roleplay packs; not the Competitive Positioning pack, buyer-bot builder, analytics, SSO or integrations.
- **Perform only:** CRM integrations (Hubspot, Salesforce, Dynamics), call recorders (Gong, Zoom, Orum) and the Live Call Coaching add-on.
- **Practice and Perform:** SSO + SCIM, Slack & Teams, data export API, EU data residency, implementation services.
- **Discounts:** volume discounts by team size and extra savings for multi-year agreements; "Contact our team for custom pricing".

## Positioning & messaging

- "Start free. Upgrade when your team is ready."
- Practice + Perform as a closed loop: rehearse until "the behavior changes", then score real calls to "verify the behavior changed"; "measurable improvement only comes from running the two together".
- Fast setup: "first bot and scorecard takes less than 10 minutes"; full setup "two weeks on average"; value "in the first 30 days".
- Financial services angle: trust, complex objections and high-stakes conversations for advisors and financial SDRs.
- Languages: "25+ languages", explicitly including Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi and Tamil.

## Where they're strong / where we win

**Where they're strong**
- Indian languages: Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi and Tamil are listed, so vernacular support alone is **not** a differentiator against them.
- Self-serve free plan and interactive demo ("No password or credit card required").
- Practice-to-performance loop: real-call scoring, deal coaching and CRM sync in Perform.
- Enterprise readiness: SSO/SCIM, data export API, implementation services, Slack/Teams.
- Deep integration with the US sales stack (Salesforce, Gong, Seismic, Highspot).

**Where Rehearsely likely wins**
- Indian BFSI and pharma depth: their banking page is about financial advisors and AUM growth (wealth management), with no Indian bank branch, insurance or NBFC scenarios and no pharma content observed.
- Field and branch reps: their product is built around call-based SDR/AE workflows and US tools; Rehearsely's mobile-first field focus likely fits Indian branch and medical reps better.
- Data residency: "EU data residency" is listed; India residency was not observed, which may matter to Indian BFSI buyers (verify before using).
- Hinglish: Hindi is listed, but code-mixed Hinglish isn't mentioned; test it head to head rather than assume.
- Commercials: per-user pricing with no public numbers versus INR pricing.

## How to handle them in a sales conversation

- Don't lead with "we support Hindi": they list Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi and Tamil. Lead with Indian BFSI and pharma scenarios and compliance instead.
- Run a head-to-head on a real Indian conversation: a Hinglish insurance renewal or a doctor detailing call. Let the realism decide.
- If they pitch Practice + Perform, ask whether the buyer's reps are on recorded calls at all. Branch and field reps often aren't, which weakens the real-call scoring half.
- Ask about India data residency; their pricing page lists EU data residency only.
- Their free plan is a real strength. Offer a fast, scoped pilot in the buyer's own scenarios and language.
- Expect "first bot in under 10 minutes". Match speed, then win on how relevant the scenarios are to Indian BFSI and pharma.
