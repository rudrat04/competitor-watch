# Awarathon — Battlecard

## Overview

Awarathon is an AI-powered coaching and training platform: learning paths and a content library, one-way video role play assessments, two-way AI simulations with customisable personas, certification, and AI-generated reports for learners, managers and leadership.

- Who it's for: enterprise sales and customer-facing teams. Product videos on the page cover medical reps ("Equip Medical Reps To Win Over Doctors") and automotive (test drive inquiries to bookings).

## Pricing & packaging

Not observed on the watched pages. The only call to action is a demo request (labelled "Get Started For Free", linking to /request-demo).

## Positioning & messaging

- "Coaching Redefined With The Power Of AI": an AI Coach "integrated across your training journey".
- Replace mirror practice: "Skip boring mirror practices", via one-way video role plays.
- Learn → Practice → Interact → Certificate as one structured journey.
- Personas "angry, distracted, price-sensitive, or informed", customisable "as per industry needs".
- Outcome claims: 45% increase in on-the-job training, 50% more learners trained, 400 training hours saved, 10% revenue growth in 4 months (the page gives no customer or context for these figures).

## Where they're strong / where we win

**Where they're strong**
- Very close to Rehearsely's use case: AI personas, objection practice, certification, and medical-rep (pharma) scenarios.
- Video role plays: one-way video assessments with AI feedback suit pitch-perfect detailing.
- Complete journey: content library, learning paths and certificates in one place.
- Manager and leadership dashboards, and "on-field feedback visibility".

**Where Rehearsely likely wins**
- Languages: no Hindi, Hinglish or vernacular support was observed on the watched pages; that's likely Rehearsely's clearest difference.
- BFSI: their watched pages show pharma and automotive examples but no banking or insurance; compliance-aware BFSI scenarios are likely a gap.
- Mobile-first field practice: not observed on their pages.
- Transparent INR pricing versus demo-only.

## How to handle them in a sales conversation

- Treat them as a direct competitor: the products overlap on personas, objection handling and certification. Differentiate on language and industry depth, not on "we have AI roleplay".
- Run a live Hinglish roleplay early in the demo (e.g. an insurance customer or a busy doctor). Their watched pages make no vernacular claims.
- Ask how their scenarios handle BFSI compliance (suitability, mis-selling disclosures); their pages show no BFSI content.
- When they show the 45% / 50% / 400 hours / 10% figures, ask whose results they are; the page doesn't say.
- Contrast their "one-way video role play" with Rehearsely's two-way conversations if that fits your demo; they also have two-way simulations, so don't claim they lack them.
- Close on transparent INR pricing; they show demo-only on these pages.
