# Second Nature — Battlecard

## Overview

Second Nature sells an AI role play training platform: AI personas that "act like real buyers" across 1:1, group and chat scenarios, combined with training content (videos, decks, quizzes) into courses, with AI scoring and feedback after every session.

- Who it's for: sales and customer-facing teams; the financial-services page targets bankers, sellers, advisors and support trainers, with insurance as the worked example.
- Free self-serve role plays (discovery call, cold calling, objection handling) on desktop.

## Pricing & packaging

Not observed on the watched pages. The page offers a demo for "pricing, and plans" questions, plus free 3–5 minute role plays.

## Positioning & messaging

- "Create any scenario, any conversation type": 1:1, group and chat.
- Speed: "Describe your scenario in freeform text or upload any content, and the AI builds a full roleplay in minutes."
- Financial services: "Confident, Compliant, and Conversational AI Training" for "high-stakes customer conversations", "delivering regulatory excellence".
- Insurance results on the page: 88% of trainers said role plays "reflected real field challenges", 85% "felt they were meaningfully challenged".
- Global: "Over 30 Languages Supported".

## Where they're strong / where we win

**Where they're strong**
- Fast authoring: generate a full roleplay from text or uploaded content.
- Try-before-you-buy: free role plays anyone can start from the website.
- Regulated-industry messaging already in place for financial services and insurance.
- Admin depth: roles, permissions, sub-accounts, and analytics-stack connections.
- Scenario variety: group and chat role plays, not just 1:1.

**Where Rehearsely likely wins**
- India-specific languages: of the 30+ languages, the page names European languages and Japanese and doesn't mention Hindi; Hindi/Hinglish personas are likely a real difference (verify, since "and more" may include them).
- Indian BFSI context: their financial-services content is generic and insurance-led, with nothing observed on Indian regulation, NBFCs or bank branch sales.
- Pharma: no pharma content on the watched pages.
- Field reps: their free role plays require "any desktop"; Rehearsely's mobile-first practice likely fits field and branch teams better.

## How to handle them in a sales conversation

- Expect them to win on "build any scenario in minutes". Show yours just as fast, then focus on what the scenario sounds like: an Indian customer speaking Hinglish.
- Ask them to demo a Hindi or Hinglish banking conversation. Their page lists 30+ languages but names none from India.
- For Indian BFSI, bring local compliance scenarios (insurance mis-selling, loan disclosures). Their financial-services page is generic and US/insurance-led.
- For pharma prospects, note that no pharma content was observed on their watched pages.
- Their free trial role plays need a desktop. Stress mobile-first practice for field and branch reps.
- Pricing: not public; lead with INR pricing.
